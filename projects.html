How I work

Start with the friction.

1 · Show me the problem

What is slow, confusing, manual, missing, expensive, or simply annoying?

2 · Define useful

We decide what a successful outcome actually looks like before building anything.

3 · Build the smallest good version

Prototype the part that creates leverage instead of decorating a giant plan.

4 · Improve from reality

Use it, learn where it bends, and make the next iteration smarter.
