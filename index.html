The photographer

I like photographs that feel lived-in.

I’m Bill Thorpe, a photographer based in Manhattan, Illinois. I photograph athletes, families, couples, businesses, products, weddings, and the occasional thing that does not fit neatly into a category.

I’m interested in expression, timing, light, and the small details that make an image feel specific. I would rather give you a frame that feels like your day than one that merely looks like everybody else’s portfolio.
